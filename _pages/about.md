I'm **Zijian Zhang**, a PhD student at the [Kavli Institute for Astronomy and Astrophysics](https://kiaa.pku.edu.cn/), [Peking University](https://www.pku.edu.cn/), advised by [Prof. Linhua Jiang](http://kavli.pku.edu.cn/~jiang/). From September 2026 to September 2027, I am visiting Prof. Roberto Maiolino's group at the [University of Cambridge](https://www.cam.ac.uk/). I received my Bachelor's degree from Nanjing University in September 2023. I'm also interested in machine learning and data-driven astrophysics.
